## 2. Key Rationale & High-Level Summary

For over a century, since Ludwig von Bertalanffy’s General System Theory, Ilya Prigogine’s Dissipative Structures, Santa Fe Institute's Complexity Economics, and Hsue-Shen Tsien’s Open Complex Giant Systems (OCGS), scientists have struggled to establish a unified axiomatic foundation for how order spontaneously emerges, persists, and evolves in entropy-driven universe.

This monograph delivers a paradigm shift by establishing **"Order as the First Principle"**:

1. **Axiomatic Foundation (Definition 1)**: *"No Observer, No World."* Introduces the absolute origin act of **Boundary Cutting** driven by Meta-Cognition ($\Phi$), transforming chaotic entropy into ordered state spaces ($\Pi$).
2. **The Physics Constitution (8 Laws)**: Derives 8 universal physics laws governing open complex giant systems, including the Decoder Mechanism for Architectural Logic Collapse, Holographic Anti-Entropy Operators, and Emergent Phase Transitions.
3. **Five-Dimensional Embodied Mind**: Integrates Space, Time, Information, Energy, and Meta-Cognition into a unified operator framework.
4. **Empirical Validation**: Proves the predictive power across four mega-scale complex systems: Global Aerospace Engineering, Global Supply Networks, Computational Biology, and Intelligent Traffic Swarms.
