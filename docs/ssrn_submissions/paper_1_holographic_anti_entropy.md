### Abstract (英文摘要)

This paper presents the *Holographic Anti-Entropy Theory*, a rigorous axiomatic framework for governing open complex giant systems facing factorial state space complexity $O(N!)$. Bridging Prigogine's dissipative structures, Qian Xuesen's open complex giant systems, Karl Friston's free energy principle, and Gödel's incompleteness, this framework overcomes the historical impasse of complex systems research—which has traditionally suffered from descriptive, open-loop, and non-computable limitations.

We introduce four fundamental mathematical operators:
1. **The Prior Boundary Operator ($\mathbf{\Pi}$)**: Performs idempotent algebraic pruning on infinite-dimensional phase space $\Xi$, projecting it onto a finite-dimensional state space $\Omega$ ($\dim(\Omega) \ll \dim(\Xi)$).
2. **The Rigid Orbit Manifold ($\mathbf{\Pi}_\bot$)**: Establishes a low-dimensional invariant manifold within $\Omega$ where orthogonal constraint forces perform negative-entropy work to neutralize spontaneous thermal noise and entropy production.
3. **The Residual Norm Difference ($\Delta$)**: Captures the norm difference $\Delta = \|\Omega_{\text{real}} - \Omega_{\text{model}}\|$, serving as the sole non-arbitrary trigger for evolutionary adaptation.
4. **The Second-Order Metacognitive Operator ($\mathbf{\Phi}$)**: Rewrites and reconstructs the basis vectors of $\mathbf{\Pi}$ when $\Delta$ exceeds critical thresholds ($\Delta > \theta_{\text{trigger}}$), executing paradigm shifts.

Empirically validated across global discrete manufacturing networks controlling 60% of world laptop production, this framework achieved closed-loop autonomous decision-making ("Human Out of the Loop"), increasing inventory turn by 1.9x, fulfillment response to 98%, and releasing billions in liquidity. This work establishes the physical constitution for computational control of complex giant systems.

### 摘要（中文摘要）

本文提出《全息抗熵理论》，针对面对阶乘级复杂度 $O(N!)$ 的开放复杂巨系统，建立了一套代数自洽的物理宪法。理论架起了普里高津耗散结构、钱学森开放复杂巨系统、Karl Friston 自由能原理与哥德尔不完备定理之间的桥梁，正面攻克了复杂性科学长期停留在“事后描述、定性解释与开环仿真”的百年历史天堑。

我们形式化定义了四大核心做功算子：
1. **先验划界算符 ($\mathbf{\Pi}$)**：对无限维相空间 $\Xi$ 执行幂等代数剪枝，将其降维投影至有限维状态空间 $\Omega$。
2. **刚性轨道流形 ($\mathbf{\Pi}_\bot$)**：在状态空间中建立低维闭合流形，利用法向约束力执行强行校正做功，抵消自发熵增。
3. **残差范数差分 ($\Delta$)**：计算实然与应然框架的范数差分 $\Delta = \|\Omega_{\text{real}} - \Omega_{\text{model}}\|$，作为系统进化的唯一非主观算子。
4. **二阶自省算符 ($\mathbf{\Phi}$)**：当残差超越临界阈值时，自动重写划界算符与刚性流形的正交基底，完成范式代际跃迁。

本体系在覆盖全球 60% 笔记本产能的千亿级制造网络中完成了工程闭环验证，实现了“人在环外、自主决策”的工业自动驾驶，交期应答率提升至 98%，库存周转率提升 1.9 倍，硬性释放数十亿流动资金。
