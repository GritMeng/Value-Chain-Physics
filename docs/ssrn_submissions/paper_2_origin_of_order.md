### Abstract (英文摘要)

This paper addresses the ultimate ontological question of system science: *How does order emerge from chaos, persist against entropy, and evolve across generations?* Applying Occam's razor to strip away intermediate domain-specific jargon, we establish the **Meta-Syntax of Order** based on three foundational axioms:

1. **Axiom of Generation (No Observer, No World)**: Order does not exist as a passive objective entity. Chaos becomes a world if and only if an observer executes a boundary-drawing action via the Prior Boundary Operator $\mathbf{\Pi}$, erecting an "Ought-To-Be" (应然) state space framework against unmodeled background reality.
2. **Axiom of Persistence (No Constraint, Dissipation)**: An Ought-To-Be framework inevitably dissipates into noise unless rigid physical constraints $\mathbf{\Pi}_\bot$ enforce invariant manifold boundaries, performing negative-entropy work to preserve systemic structural integrity.
3. **Axiom of Evolution (No Residual, Stagnation)**: Because any Ought-To-Be framework is a finite projection of infinite reality, systemic evolution depends strictly on monitoring the Residual Difference $\Delta = \|\Omega_{\text{real}} - \Omega_{\text{model}}\|$. Without residual feedback, frameworks solidify into dogma and evolutionary iteration halts.

We demonstrate that all human constructs—from intuitive commonsense, religious doctrines, and philosophical paradigms, to quantum measurement, Shannon information theory, and physical AI decision engines—are domain-specific projections of this single Meta-Syntax. We establish a complete formal dictionary mapping Daoist boundary-drawing, Kantian synthetic a priori, Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form*, Friston's free energy principle, and Longbing Cao's Non-IID complexity to our three fundamental axioms.

### 摘要（中文摘要）

本文回答了系统科学最原初的本体论追问：*一切有序结构——无论是知识、信息、系统、哲学还是科学——如何从无序中诞生、在噪声中存续、并在残差中进化？*

我们拿起奥卡姆剃刀，剔除所有中间预设，建立了由三大公理构成的《秩序元语法》：
1. **生成公理（无观察者则无世界）**：无序未经划分，则世界无从成立。观察者通过划界算子 $\mathbf{\Pi}$ 划分界限，建立“应然”状态空间框架。
2. **存续公理（无约束则耗散）**：应然框架若无自洽规则与刚性轨道流形 $\mathbf{\Pi}_\bot$ 的法向约束做功，框架内部要素必因无序对抗而归于热力学耗散。
3. **进化公理（无残差则停滞）**：应然框架面对无限实然背景必然具备局限性。若不识别与计算残差 $\Delta$，框架即固化为教条，进化就此停滞。

本文给出了该元语法在东方哲学（老庄划界、心学良知）、西方哲学（康德立法、斯佩塞-布朗形式法则）、量子物理（惠勒参与式宇宙）、生物物理（薛定谔负熵、Friston自由能）与操龙兵 Non-IID 复杂性中的 1:1 同构字典，为一切人类建构物提供了通用、可证伪的第一性原理源代码。
