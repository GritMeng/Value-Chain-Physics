### 1. Data Availability Statement

The datasets, topological network structures, and computational benchmark scenarios analyzed in this study originate from 18 years of industrial physical practice in global supply chain systems (Lenovo Group IPS System) and open-source complexity models. 

* **Public Repository**: All core topological data structures, algorithm pseudo-code, mathematical proof verifications, and benchmark models supporting the conclusions of this paper are deposited and publicly accessible in the open-source GitHub repository:  
  [https://github.com/GritMeng/Value-Chain-Physics](https://github.com/GritMeng/Value-Chain-Physics)
* **Preprints and Extended Documentation**: SSRN Working Papers (Abstract IDs: 7451920, 7442100, 7251098).
* **Proprietary Data**: Any specific confidential commercial transactions have been fully anonymized into normalized topological graph matrices in the open repository to ensure complete reproducibility without violating commercial non-disclosure obligations.
