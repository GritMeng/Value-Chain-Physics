### Author Biography

**Grit Meng (Fanchun Meng)** is the former Head and Chief Architect of Lenovo's Global Supply Chain Integrated Planning Solution (IPS) System and the creator of the IPC (Intelligent Planning and Control) Engine. He has led the architecture and deployment of industrial planning engines across global lighthouse manufacturing networks (including LCFC, WEF Lighthouse Factory). His academic research focuses on complex systems science, value chain physics, non-IID learning, and holographic meta-cognitive mind models. He is the author of several monographs including *Value Chain Physics* and *Systems and Complexity Science: Order Generation, Survival, and Evolution*. Currently, he serves as Chief Architect at the Department of Global Digital Neural System Design, Shenzhen, China.
