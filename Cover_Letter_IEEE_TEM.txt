### Background & Core Problem Addressed
Over the past two decades, global manufacturing enterprises have invested billions of dollars in enterprise digital transformation (ERP, APS, MES, WMS, control towers). However, average Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) in manufacturing has persistently stagnated or declined, resurrecting Brynjolfsson's classic "IT Productivity Paradox" in the era of industrial intelligence. 

We argue that the root cause lies in a fundamental paradigm mismatch: traditional enterprise architecture tools implicitly assume "Independent and Identically Distributed (IID)" weak coupling among functional departments, whereas physical manufacturing value networks are intrinsically Non-IID, high-dimensional, strongly-coupled Open Complex Giant Systems (OCGS), as originally conceptualized by Qian Xuesen.

### Key Contributions & Innovations
This paper bridges Systems Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Enterprise Architecture to make four foundational contributions:
1. **Value Chain Physics & 5D State-Space Manifold**: We construct a Systemically Complete and Irreducible (SCI) 5D topological manifold M(t) = <N, T, C, x(t), Delta x(t)>, proving that departmental non-orthogonal KPI friction generates Vector Cancellation and internal dissipative heat Delta W_heat, eroding ROIC.
2. **Complexity Reduction ($O(N!) \to O(N \log N)$)**: We propose a 5D Double-Helix Architecture combining a Holographic Data Model and Dynamic Evolution Algorithms. Through Prior Partitioning (Pi) and Rigid Manifold Operators (Pi_bot), we achieve algebraic pruning from factorial O(N!) search spaces to polynomial O(N log N) scalability.
3. **Formal Proofs of Qian Xuesen's OCGS Principles**: We provide rigorous mathematical proofs for the necessary and sufficient conditions of Qian Xuesen's three core OCGS principles (Overall Design Department, Hall of Workshop for Decision Support, and Human-Machine Integration with Human-in-the-Lead).
4. **10-Year Field Empirical Validation ($100B Enterprise Network)**: We report a 10-year longitudinal field deployment (2015–2024; audited 2014–2024 financial records) across Lenovo Group's global manufacturing network (including the WEF Lighthouse Factory in Hefei). Empirical results and rigorous ablation studies confirm that closed-loop autonomous decision write-back achieves 98.4% On-Time Delivery (OTD), 24.6 inventory turns/year (+1.9x), and releases over RMB 3.5 billion in Net Working Capital (NWC).
